Two quick answers

Which plan is right for me?

If you use Evernote on your own, Personal gives you everything Free is missing: unlimited devices, offline access, AI-powered search, PDF annotation and full tasks & reminders. If you work with a team and need team collaboration and admin & user management, choose Professional.

How is Professional priced?

€14.99 per month for each seat, meaning each person on your team who uses it.
